# Opening Crawl

Open your session like a movie. Write a Star Wars style opening crawl, give it your own fonts, colors, background and music, and play it full screen for everyone at the table.

![Foundry v13](https://img.shields.io/badge/Foundry-v13-informational) ![Foundry v14](https://img.shields.io/badge/Foundry-v14-informational)

## Features

- An intro line, a big campaign title that zooms away, then the episode title, subtitle and the scrolling text
- 134 bundled fonts, with a separate font and color for each part of the crawl
- Your own background image, or a star field
- A music track with its own volume that fades out when the crawl ends
- Sliders for scroll duration, tilt angle, text size and campaign title size
- A live preview next to the editor that updates while you type
- Save crawls to a library in your world and load them again later
- Preview a crawl just for yourself, or play it for all connected players. The GM gets a Stop button that ends it for everyone

All fonts ship with the module, so nothing is loaded from the internet. Music and images are not included: pick your own files from your Foundry data.

## Installation

In Foundry, open **Add-on Modules > Install Module** and paste this manifest URL:

```
https://github.com/The-Final-Artificer/hqp-opening-crawl/releases/latest/download/module.json
```

Then enable **Opening Crawl** in your world's Manage Modules.

Manual alternative: download `module.zip` from the latest release and extract it into your Foundry `Data/modules/hqp-opening-crawl` folder.

## Usage

1. As GM, open the **Journal Notes** controls and click the **Opening Crawl** button.
2. Fill in the text, pick fonts and colors, and set a background and music if you want them.
3. Click **Preview** to watch it yourself, or **Play for All** to show it to everyone.
4. To keep a crawl, type a name in **Save as...** and click **Save**.
